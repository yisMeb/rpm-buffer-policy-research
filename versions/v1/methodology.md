# Methodology
## Design
This source-informed, local, discrete-event comparative simulation uses paired synthetic input sequences and connectivity traces for P1–P4. Main configuration is frozen in `config/experiments.yaml` before the main run. Each capacity × outage × throughput × condition × seed cell has four policies. The independent stochastic unit is one seed within a cell, never one reading. A three hour acquisition window is followed by 2.5 hours of recovery. AoI is measured during acquisition, eventual delivery and episode metrics include the recovery tail. Pending counts are reported explicitly.

Acquisition occurs every five seconds. Synthetic baseline heart rate, oxygen saturation and temperature have smooth cycles plus seeded noise. Poisson episode counts and uniformly sampled starts are independent of outage start, duration and policy. Durations vary by condition, episodes may occur before, during or after outages by chance. Severity is one of three perturbations: increased heart rate, reduced SpO2 or increased temperature. Experimental abnormality thresholds are heart rate < 50 or > 100 bpm, SpO2 <92%, or temperature > 38°C. These thresholds are modeling assumptions, not diagnostic criteria. Overlapping episodes resolve to the earliest sampled active episode. A seed may have zero abnormal episodes, episode ratios then remain missing rather than being counted as success.

Low, medium and high conditions have Poisson means of 0.5, 1.5 and 3 episodes perhour. Duration ranges are 30–90, 45–180, 60–240 seconds. These span sparse short episodes to denser longer ones for sensitivity analysis, they are not estimated from patients.

One outage begins 30 minutes into acquisition. Duration is 0, 5, 15, 30, 60 or 120 minutes. The model supports repeated outages through `network_repeats`, though the frozen main factorial run uses one outage. Network becomes usable five seconds after an outage ends. While connected, one completion slot occurs every 1/rate seconds. Rates 1, 5 and 10 readings/s are modeled capacities, not measured ESP32, MQTT or AWS rates. Every completed transmission succeeds. No packet corruption, duplicated delivery or server-side outage is modeled.

At equal times, a transmission slot precedes acquisition. P1 sends newly acquired reading immediately if connected, else discards it. P2 to P4 always first enqueue and use the next service slot. All buffered policies use the same capacity and service clock. P2 rejects a new reading on full queue. P3 removes the oldest. P4 removes the oldest routine reading when full, if all stored readings are abnormal, it rejects a routine arrival and replaces the oldest abnormal reading with an abnormal arrival. All retained readings are sent FIFO in acquisition order. Classification uses only measurement-time values.

AoI(t)=t−max acquisition timestamp delivered by t, an older late delivery never rolls back freshness. A virtual timestamp zero update defines the initial age as zero. The continuous time mean is the integral divided by the acquisition horizon. Median and p95 are quantiles of time spent at each age, peak is the largest pre update age. Overall and stratum delivery ratios use generated readings as denominators. Episode preservation means at least one reading delivered, complete/lost fractions and mean within-episode fraction are separate. Delay is first corresponding delivery minus first generated episode measurement time, conditional on detection, deadline rates use all generated episodes. Detection here is a delivery event, not clinical diagnosis. Losses include rejection and eviction. Backlog recovery time is seconds from reconnection to first empty queue.

Per scenario means and approximate 95% normal intervals use seed-level run values and actual nonmissing counts. P4 paired differences first average matched scenario differences within each seed, then use 2,000 bootstrap resamples of the 30 seed clusters(bootstrap seed 2026). This preserves dependence from reusing a seed across scenario cells. Pooling scenario cells gives a descriptive average over this balanced design, not a population estimate. Missing episode ratios are excluded only from corresponding comparisons and are counted in source run data.
